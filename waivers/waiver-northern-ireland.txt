1. Permission to Camp
The landowner (“the Farmer”) grants permission for the camper(s) named above (“the Camper”) to pitch a tent and camp in the specified area for the agreed dates only.
This is wild camping — no facilities or services are provided. The land is not a campsite. The Camper must be fully self-sufficient and remove all equipment, litter, and waste upon departure.
This permission is personal to the Camper and may not be transferred, sublet, or used for organised events, gatherings, or commercial activities.

2. Legal Status and Applicable Law
By signing this agreement, the Camper confirms that they understand they are entering the land with the landowner’s permission, and that the landowner’s duty of care is governed by the Occupiers’ Liability Acts 1957 and 1984 (as applicable in the UK jurisdiction).
This agreement is governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of [England and Wales / Scotland / Northern Ireland], and any dispute shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the relevant courts.

3. Acknowledgement of Risk
The Camper understands and accepts that:
* Farmland may contain inherent risks and hazards, including uneven ground, livestock, watercourses, electric fences, ditches, farm machinery, and vegetation.
* The land is not inspected or adapted for public camping and no amenities (toilets, water, lighting, waste disposal) are provided.
* The area is remote and unsupervised; mobile coverage may be limited.
* The Camper is responsible for their own safety, health, and belongings.
* Camping is undertaken entirely at the Camper’s own risk.

4. Conduct and Safety Rules
The Camper agrees to:
* Follow Leave No Trace principles at all times.
* No open fires or barbecues unless specifically permitted in writing by the Farmer.
* Keep a minimum safe distance (e.g., 50 metres) from livestock, buildings, and watercourses.
* Respect boundaries, signs, and any verbal or written instructions.
* Keep dogs under strict control and on a lead at all times.
* Report immediately any injury, incident, or damage.
* Depart the site as found, leaving no litter or materials.

5. Liability and Indemnity
The Camper confirms that:
* They understand the nature of wild camping and accept all associated risks.
* They agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Farmer against any claim, loss, or damage arising from their stay, except where injury or loss results from the Farmer’s negligence or wilful misconduct, as defined by applicable law.
* This agreement does not constitute a tenancy or grant of legal interest in the land.

6. Data Protection
The Farmer may retain this form for safety, recordkeeping, and insurance purposes only. Information will be securely stored and not shared except where required by law or for insurance/incident reporting.

7. Severability
If any part of this agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full effect.
