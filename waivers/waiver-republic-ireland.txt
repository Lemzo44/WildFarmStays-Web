1. Permission to Camp
The landowner ('the Farmer') grants permission for the camper(s) named above ('the Camper') to pitch a tent and camp in the specified area for the agreed dates only.
This is wild camping - no amenities or services are provided. The land is not a campsite. The Camper must be completely self-sufficient and leave no trace of their stay.
This permission is personal to the Camper and may not be transferred, sublet, or used for organised events, gatherings, or commercial activity.

2. Status and Applicable Law
The Camper is permitted access as a 'recreational user' under the Occupiers' Liability Act 1995 (Ireland).
This agreement is governed by and interpreted according to the laws of Ireland, and any dispute shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the Irish courts.

3. Acknowledgement of Risk
The Camper understands and accepts that:
* The land is working farmland, with natural and man-made hazards including uneven terrain, livestock, electric fences, ditches, wells, streams, and machinery.
* The area is not inspected or prepared for public camping use.
* No facilities such as toilets, water, lighting, or waste disposal are provided.
* Camping is unsupervised and remote, with limited mobile coverage.
* The Camper is responsible for their own safety, health, and possessions.
* The Camper enters and stays entirely at their own risk.

4. Conduct and Safety Rules
The Camper agrees to:
* Follow 'Leave No Trace' principles.
* No open fires, barbecues, or stoves unless the Farmer gives written permission.
* Keep at least 50 metres from livestock, water sources, and farm buildings.
* Respect all boundaries, signage, and instructions from the Farmer.
* Keep dogs under full control and on a lead.
* Report immediately any incident, injury, or damage.
* Depart the site in the same or better condition as found.

5. Liability and Indemnity
The Camper:
* Confirms that they understand the nature of wild camping and voluntarily accept all related risks.
* Agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Farmer from any claims, losses, or damages arising from their stay, except where injury or loss results from the Farmer's reckless disregard or deliberate act, as defined in the Occupiers' Liability Act 1995.
* Acknowledges that this permission does not constitute a tenancy or lease and grants no ongoing rights of access.

6. Data Protection
The Farmer may retain this form for insurance and safety purposes only. Information will be stored securely and not shared with third parties except where legally required or for insurance claims.

7. Severability
If any part of this agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
